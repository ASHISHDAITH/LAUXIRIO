# LAUXIRIO media art direction

## Creative objective

Build a coherent, dark-luxury, unisex salon campaign that feels editorial rather than like a collage of unrelated stock photography. Media must communicate craft, texture, hospitality and a real physical studio without inventing salon facts or presenting illustrative models as LAUXIRIO people.

## Visual system

- **Palette:** deep espresso, ink, charcoal and warm stone; controlled champagne highlights. Preserve natural hair and skin colour; avoid orange casts, crushed blacks and metallic-looking skin.
- **Light:** sculptural, directional key light with soft falloff and deliberate separation from the background. Keep specular highlights on hair controlled and detailed.
- **Composition:** editorial negative space for headings and calls to action. Keep eyes, hair shape and stylist hands inside mobile-safe crops. Avoid text baked into imagery.
- **Texture:** retain pores, flyaways, curls and natural hair detail. Avoid excessive smoothing, artificial shine and over-sharpening.
- **Colour grade:** one restrained warm-neutral grade across the collection. Do not force identical exposure or skin tones; consistency should come from contrast, white balance and highlight treatment.
- **Styling:** contemporary, timeless, inclusive and unisex. Coordinate wardrobe and set dressing with the dark-neutral identity; no visible third-party salon branding or unapproved products.
- **Variety:** vary people, hair types, lengths, curl patterns, skin tones, framing and studio details. Do not reuse the same photograph across unrelated service/lookbook placements merely to fill the grid.

## Capture and selection

Prefer an owner-approved campaign shoot at the real studio. Capture final results, process, consultation, team portraits and architectural details in the same production. If stock is considered, select only specific assets after checking source pages, creator details, commercial rights, releases, trademarks, attribution/credit terms and owner approval. Do not use editorial-only media for commercial site promotion.

Artist profiles must use genuine staff photography and approved names/bios. Until those are supplied, keep artist images clearly illustrative and do not present stock models as team members. Similarly, studio photographs must show the actual LAUXIRIO space before being described as such.

## Delivery and performance

- Keep clean, high-resolution originals and license/release documentation outside the public directory.
- Deliver optimized AVIF/WebP images at dimensions appropriate to their layout; retain a high-quality hero still for desktop and a deliberate mobile crop when the owner supplies one.
- Use local files from the existing `public/media/` category directories for approved assets.
- Prefer lazy-loaded responsive images below the fold; keep the first-screen hero poster prioritized. Prevent layout shifts with known intrinsic dimensions.
- Optional campaign film should be silent, seamless, 8-15 seconds, with realistic hair movement, stylist craft, a true salon interior and a poster image. Keep the existing image fallback and do not configure video until the real licensed file exists.
- Run `npm run audit:media` after each asset/config change and visually review crops, colour and load behavior on mobile and desktop.

## Rights and representation

An asset is not approved merely because a provider allows downloads or because it loads successfully. Preserve the original asset page, creator, applicable license, acquisition date, releases/consents, intended uses and approval. Respect editorial-only restrictions and limitations on trademarks, identifiable people, implied endorsement and redistribution. Never scrape competitor assets or use watermarked previews. No paid subscription or purchase is authorized by this phase.
